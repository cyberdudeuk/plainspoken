# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to:

- be respectful, inclusive and patient, whatever someone's background, role or level of experience
- assume good intent and give feedback on the work, not the person
- never share personal data or confidential information about anyone
- follow their own organisation's code of conduct where it applies

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not accepted. Maintainers may hide, edit or remove contributions that break
this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

Raise a concern by [opening an issue](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/plainspoken/issues/new/choose). No email address is
published: contact is through GitHub only.
