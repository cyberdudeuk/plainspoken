# Privacy

- **Stored:** settings, event briefs, transcripts you type or dictate, scores and logs.
- **Where:** Claude version: a private per-user store on claude.ai plus your browser. GitHub Pages version: your browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent to the author.
- **Claude features:** coaching, brief review and role-play send the relevant text to Claude (Anthropic) to generate a reply, only when you ask.
- **Live listening (Pages version):** Chrome and Edge send audio to the browser maker's speech service. Safari may process on the device.
- **Do not enter** confidential or commercially sensitive information, or personal data about other people.
- **No cookies, analytics or tracking.** Browser storage is used only to provide the features you ask for. Erase all data in Library → Settings.
