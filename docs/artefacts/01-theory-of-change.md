# Theory of change: Plainspoken

> **How to read this.** Facts marked V were read from this repository on 20 November 2026. Assumptions, measures and risks are proposals written for this kit, not findings. U means unconfirmed. Where another document in this repository already covers a topic, this one links to it rather than repeating it.

## Problem
Practising speaking for work is easy to skip without a daily routine, feedback and visible progress.

## Long-term outcome
Users practise daily and feel more confident in meetings, talks and briefings.

## Chain

| Inputs | Activities | Outputs | Short-term outcomes | Medium-term outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A 16-week articulation plan; 16 workplace speaking situations | Daily 20-minute routine; event prep; speak-and-score; role-play; live cue card; rewards | index.html built by build.py; a Teams package; a Claude artifact version | Users keep a practice rhythm | Users report more confidence at real events |

## Assumptions (proposed; each one testable)

| # | Assumption | How to test it | Evidence today |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Rewards keep the habit going | Owner's own use for 4 weeks, then 3 others | Not tested |

## Measures (proposed)
- Practice rhythm kept for 4 weeks (owner use)
- Checks pass (check.yml)

No target is set before a baseline exists.

## What would prove this wrong
If the rhythm drops in week 2, simplify to the cue card and routine only.
