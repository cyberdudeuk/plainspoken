# Roadmap

Ideas for taking Plainspoken further, grouped by theme. Open an issue to discuss or vote.

## Listening and feedback
- Record each answer on the Pages version and play it back; clips stay on the device
- Live loudness and pitch-range trace with a monotone warning (Web Audio API)
- Measure silent pauses from audio, not text, and reward deliberate ones
- Word-level pace heatmap over the transcript
- On-device speech recognition where browsers support it, so audio never leaves the phone

## Learning science
- Spaced repetition for STAR examples, introductions and key lines
- Adaptive daily quests that target your weakest measure
- Difficulty ladders for role-plays (grade-specific question banks, interruptions, time pressure)
- Personalised drill recommendations from your score history

## Motivation (without dark patterns)
- Opt-in team challenges with a shared resonance goal rather than rankings
- Rhythm repair by completing an extra practice the next day
- Monthly reflection: "then vs now" side-by-side of baseline and latest recordings
- Optional sound effects and haptics

## Skills and facilitation
- Printable course pack (learner and facilitator) generated from the skill lessons
- Shared facilitator view: a facilitator scores a learner's final task against the rubric on their own device
- Spaced review of quick-check questions you got wrong

## Real-world integration
- Calendar link: start a brief from an upcoming meeting, reminder the day before
- Daily reminders via a Claude scheduled task, or notifications on the Pages version
- Shared practice rooms: a partner joins as Ah-Counter or interviewer with scores on both screens
- Coach's notes from a manager or mentor, linked to sessions
- Export a Success Profiles evidence pack from the situation log

## Platform
- Installable progressive web app with offline drills (Pages version)
- Sync between Claude and Pages versions
- Welsh language prompts and coaching; British Sign Language resources for audiences
- Automated browser tests (Playwright) in GitHub Actions
- Accessibility audit against WCAG 2.2 AA
